### 观察 1

**标签**：机床业
**日期**：2026-10-12
**标题**：机床工具行业 1–8 月账本：切削在涨、成形在跌，钱从钣金流向铣削

**摘要**：中国机床工具工业协会公布 2026 年 1–8 月运行数据：全行业营业收入 7209 亿元、同比 +7.5%，但内部分化得很厉害 —— 金属切削机床分行业 **+18.6%**，金属成形机床分行业 **−4.2%**。需求端，金属加工机床新增订单 +15.7%、在手订单 +19.3%；供给端，全国规模以上企业金属切削机床产量 58.4 万台、+8.8%，金属成形机床产量 12.5 万台、+7.8%。进出口总额 231.5 亿美元、+5.7%，其中出口 158.8 亿美元、+5.0%，但出口结构是往下走的：金属切削机床出口 39.5 亿美元、**−2.3%**，金属成形机床出口 21.5 亿美元、−1.7%；进口端金属切削机床 31.8 亿美元、−0.3%，金属成形机床进口 3.5 亿美元、−14.7%。

**对零件意味着什么**：切削机床在涨、成形机床在跌，说明下游正在把原本交给冲压和折弯做的零件往铣削一侧搬 —— 对送件给 CNC 厂的客户来说，接下来会越来越多地遇到「批量不大但形状复杂」的件，而这类件开模不划算，只能靠小批量快速打样跑出来。

**来源**：中国机床工具工业协会《2026年1-8月机床工具行业经济运行简讯》（cmtba.org.cn，2026-10-12）

### 观察 2（本期 AI 主线）

**标签**：AI / 制造
**日期**：2026-10-12
**标题**：AI 与系统集成第一次被写成硬指标：上海要 2000 个场景、1000 亿集成规模，全国智能工厂底数同时摊开

**摘要**：10 月 12 日工博会开幕式上，工信部总工程师王卫明表示要「纵深推进『人工智能+制造』，大力发展开源基座模型和垂类模型」；同日「上海市加快发展新一代智能制造行动」正式启动，四大工程、19 项重点任务。上海给出的 2028 年硬指标值得逐条记下：重点制造企业智能工厂全覆盖，培育 500 家先进级智能工厂，**人工智能在制造业应用场景突破 2000 个**，开发 **10 个**先进适用的垂域大模型，推出 **300 个**高水平智能体；工业机器人密度达每万人 **600 台**，推动 **8000 台人形机器人进工厂**，AI 赋能的新型智能制造装备产业规模突破 **2000 亿元**，**智能制造系统集成产业规模达到 1000 亿元**（现为超 600 亿元，智能制造装备产值超 1500 亿元）。全国底数同场摊开：截至 2026 年上半年，我国累计建成 5.6 万余家基础级、1.3 万余家先进级、500 余家卓越级智能工厂，培育 15 家领航级；**AI 已融入领航级智能工厂 70% 以上业务场景**，沉淀超 6000 个垂直领域模型。另有第三方口径：国家工业信息安全发展研究中心《制造业数智化转型能力水平（2026）》显示，AI 技术重点场景应用普及率 34.2%（较上年增长 5.5 个百分点），10.6% 的规上制造业企业已用大模型赋能业务革新。车间一侧同样有实例——黑湖科技（服务超 3 万家工厂）展出 9 款决策型工业智能体，其中工程 Agent 可在约 1 分钟完成图纸工艺解析、准确率 95%。

**对零件意味着什么**：工程 Agent 一分钟把图纸读成工艺，意味着「图纸说不清」第一次变成立竿见影的成本——AI 读不懂的标注，人也要来回问，报价自然慢；而上海把「智能制造系统集成产业规模」定成 1000 亿元目标，说明将来下单给你的人会越来越多是系统集成商而不是终端工厂，他们比的是成套交付的确定性，不是单件价格。

**来源**：上海市人民政府官网 2026-10-13《上海将实施四大工程 推动人工智能与制造业深度融合 到2028年 8000台人形机器人进工厂》；《经济参考报》（新华社）2026-10-13；人民网上海频道 2026-10-12（工信部装备工业一司副司长叶猛致辞）；中国金融信息网 2026-10-13；同花顺行业频道 2026-10-12（引国家工业信息安全发展研究中心《制造业数智化转型能力水平（2026）》、弗若斯特沙利文《2026年中国工业智能体市场白皮书》）。

### 观察 3

**标签**：液冷 / 算力
**日期**：2026-10-09
**标题**：液冷第一次在财报上兑现，而不只是躺在订单簿上

**摘要**：鸿富瀚发布 2026 年前三季度业绩预告：净利润 1.70 亿–2.00 亿元、同比 +108.19%~144.93%；其中第三季度单季 0.97 亿–1.27 亿元，对比 Q2 的 0.34 亿元、环比 **+182%~270%**。公司在公告里把增量归因写得很直接：「重点布局的液冷板整柜模组业务完成量产交付，液冷板模组产能逐步释放，成为本期利润增长的核心增量」。同一批业绩预告里，景旺电子 Q3 归母净利润 9.12 亿–10.89 亿元、同比 +205.46%~264.74%，增量来自高速通信与 AI 数据基础设施领域高性能 PCB 量产出货提速。

**对零件意味着什么**：液冷板整柜模组是这条链上标准化程度最高的一段（冷板、机箱、支架、管路接头），以铝合金为主、批量中等、精度中高、对阳极氧化与导电处理要求严 —— 如果你的液冷零件还在排队等认证，现在补图纸还赶得上下一轮扩产，因为认证链长、交付滞后的是 CDU / UQD 那一端，模组端已经在量产交付了。

**来源**：鸿富瀚（301086.SZ）2026 年前三季度业绩预告（2026-10-09）；景旺电子（603228.SH）业绩预告（2026-10-09）；财联社、上海证券报、南方财经网同日报道。

### 观察 4

**标签**：液冷 / 供应链
**日期**：2026-10-08
**标题**：液冷从「供部件」升级为「联合定义」——瑞声科技加入英特尔中国液冷创新加速计划

**摘要**：据《科技日报》报道，在 2026 英特尔技术创新与产业生态大会上，瑞声科技与英特尔签署液冷生态合作协议，正式加入英特尔中国数据中心液冷创新加速计划，成为该计划的重要生态共创伙伴。双方合作方向包括但不限于 CPU 冷板、内存液冷、SSD 液冷、UQD/QD 连接、歧管、CDU、整机节点级液冷架构、机架级液冷方案及相关验证能力建设。关键在于合作形态的变化：瑞声不只是提供关键部件，而是从技术趋势、产品路线、系统集成到验证体系更早介入，与英特尔及生态伙伴**联合定义、联合开发、联合验证**。此外，其子公司远地科技在第六届 AI 数据中心液冷供应链峰会暨电力绿色能源大会上获「年度全场景液冷产品技术创新与解决方案奖」。

**来源**：《科技日报》2026-10-08《瑞声科技加入英特尔中国液冷创新加速计划》（stdaily.com）

**标签**：机器人 / 准入
**日期**：2026-10-10
**标题**：人形机器人开始「持证上岗」，认证压力会一路压到零件层

**摘要**：据《经济日报》报道，工厂里已有人形机器人正式上岗，承担物料补给、分拣摆盘、码垛打包等工作；2026 年国内人形机器人赛道进入产业化落地的关键窗口期，安全问题随之被摆到台面。国家认监委等多部门发布实施的中国机器人认证（简称 CR 认证），被描述为「世界范围内唯一由国家推动的自愿性高端认证」。以拿下国内首张人形机器人 CR 认证证书的智元远征 A2 为例，要经机械电气安全测试、功能安全评估、运行可靠性试验等多维度标准化检测。市场监管总局表示，下一步将前瞻布局集成电路、新能源、生物医药、**人形机器人**等战略性新兴产业的高能级质检技术平台。

**来源**：《经济日报》/中国经济网 2026-10-10《检验检测，为经济发展添动能》（引上海电器科学研究所机器人检测认证中心主任朱晓鹏、市场监管总局）；智元机器人。

## Five stories

### Story 1

**Tag**: Machine tools
**Date**: 2026-10-12
**Title**: The machine-tool industry's eight-month ledger: cutting up, forming down, and the money moving from sheet metal to milling

**Summary**: CMTBA published January–August 2026 operating data: industry-wide revenue of RMB 720.9 billion, up 7.5% year on year, but sharply split inside — the metal-cutting machine sub-sector grew **18.6%** while the metal-forming machine sub-sector fell **4.2%**. On demand, new orders for metal-cutting machine tools rose 15.7% and the order backlog rose 19.3%. On supply, above-scale producers turned out 584,000 metal-cutting machines (+8.8%) and 125,000 metal-forming machines (+7.8%). Total machine-tool imports and exports reached USD 23.15 billion (+5.7%), with exports at USD 15.88 billion (+5.0%) — but the export mix drifted down: metal-cutting machine exports were USD 3.95 billion (**-2.3%**) and metal-forming exports USD 2.15 billion (-1.7%); imports of metal-cutting machines were USD 3.18 billion (-0.3%) and metal-forming imports USD 350 million (-14.7%).

**What it means for your parts**: Cutting machines rising while forming machines fall tells you downstream is moving parts that used to be stamped or bent over to the milling side — so if you send parts to a CNC shop, expect more and more "small volume, complex shape" work, and that is exactly the category where tooling is not worth the money and only fast small-batch prototyping gets you to a decision.

**Source**: CMTBA, "Machine-tool industry economic performance, January-August 2026" (cmtba.org.cn), 2026-10-12

### Story 2 (this issue's AI lead)

**Tag**: AI / Manufacturing
**Date**: 2026-10-12
**Title**: AI and system integration get hard targets for the first time: Shanghai names 2,000 use cases and a RMB 100bn integration industry, while China's smart-factory baseline lands on the table

**Summary**: At the CIIF opening on 12 October, MIIT chief engineer Wang Weiming said the ministry would "advance AI plus manufacturing in depth, and vigorously develop open-source foundation models and vertical models." The same day, Shanghai's action plan for a new generation of intelligent manufacturing formally launched — four major programmes, 19 key tasks. Shanghai's 2028 targets are worth writing down one by one: full smart-factory coverage across the city's key manufacturers, 500 advanced-level smart factories, **more than 2,000 AI use cases in manufacturing**, **10** deployable vertical large models, **300** high-level agents; industrial robot density of **600 per 10,000 workers**, **8,000 humanoid robots working in factories**, the AI-enabled new intelligent manufacturing equipment industry passing **RMB 200 billion**, and the **intelligent manufacturing system-integration industry reaching RMB 100 billion** (from more than RMB 60 billion today, alongside equipment output above RMB 150 billion). The national baseline was laid out in the same room: as of H1 2026 China had built 56,000-plus basic-level, 13,000-plus advanced-level and 500-plus excellence-level smart factories and cultivated 15 leading-level ones, while **AI now covers more than 70% of business scenarios in leading-level smart factories** and more than 6,000 vertical-domain models have been accumulated. A separate reading from the National Industrial Information Security Development Research Centre puts AI adoption in key manufacturing scenarios at 34.2%, up 5.5 points year on year, with 10.6% of above-scale manufacturers already using large models to reshape operations. On the shop floor itself, Black Lake Technology — serving more than 30,000 factories — exhibited nine decision-making industrial agents, one of which parses a drawing into a manufacturing process in about one minute at 95% accuracy.

**What it means for your parts**: An engineering agent that turns a drawing into a process in a minute means "the drawing is not clear" becomes an immediate, visible cost for the first time — what AI cannot read, a human also has to phone you about, and the quote slows down; and with Shanghai targeting a RMB 100 billion system-integration industry, the party placing orders with you will increasingly be a system integrator rather than the end factory, buying on certainty of complete-kit delivery rather than unit price.

**Source**: Shanghai Municipal People's Government, 2026-10-13; Economic Information Daily (Xinhua), 2026-10-13; People's Daily Online (Shanghai), 2026-10-12 (remarks by Ye Meng, MIIT Department of Equipment Industry I); China Financial Information Network, 2026-10-13; Tonghuashun industry desk, 2026-10-12 (citing the National Industrial Information Security Development Research Centre and Frost & Sullivan).

### Story 5

**Tag**: Robotics / Market access
**Date**: 2026-10-10
**Title**: Humanoid robots start carrying certificates — and that pressure will travel down to the part level

**Summary**: Economic Daily reports that humanoid robots are now formally on the job in factories, handling material replenishment, sorting, palletising and packing; 2026 is described as the key window in which China's humanoid sector moves into industrial deployment, which has pushed safety to the front. The China Robot Certification (CR certification), issued and implemented by the State Certification and Accreditation Administration together with other authorities, is described as "the only state-driven voluntary high-end certification in the world." Taking AgiBot's Expedition A2 — holder of China's first humanoid CR certificate — as the example, passing requires standardised testing across mechanical and electrical safety, functional safety assessment and operational reliability trials. The State Administration for Market Regulation says it will next build out high-level quality-inspection technology platforms for strategic emerging industries including integrated circuits, new energy, biomedicine and **humanoid robots**.

**What it means for your parts**: To move a whole robot through certification, integrators will push traceability requirements all the way down to the part level — material certificates (such as EN 10204 3.1), heat-treatment batch numbers and inspection reports each tied one-to-one to a part batch — so building the habit of one traceability file per batch now is far cheaper than assembling the paperwork on the eve of certification.

**Source**: Economic Daily / ce.cn, "Testing and certification add momentum to economic development", 2026-10-10 (citing Zhu Xiaopeng, director of the robot testing and certification centre at Shanghai Electrical Apparatus Research Institute, and the State Administration for Market Regulation); AgiBot.
