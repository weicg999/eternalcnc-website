### 五条观察

#### 1. 【机器人】2026-09-15 ｜ 8 月工业机器人 +34.6%，但真正的信号藏在减速器里

**摘要**
国家统计局 9 月 15 日发布 8 月工业生产数据：工业机器人产量同比增长 **34.6%**，机器人减速器产量同比增长 **11.4%**。两条曲线在此前是同向的——2025 年减速器产量增长 63.9%，2026 年上半年还有 57.3%，都显著快于同期工业机器人 28.0% 的增速。而 7 月、8 月减速器增速分别回落到 22.7% 和 11.4%，同期整机增速反而从 30.2% 升到 34.6%。即便剔除基数影响，8 月减速器两年平均增速约 19.7%，仍低于整机的 24.1%。国家统计局同时披露，我国人形机器人出货量全球占比已提升到 **97%**；工信部预计全年人形机器人整机产量有望突破 10 万台，全年规上工业机器人产量有望首次突破 100 万台。

**对零件意味着什么**
减速器是关节里唯一"必须成对交付"的精密件——壳体、柔轮、刚轮、输出法兰，一套四件，任何一件尺寸漂了整套装不上。它的产量增速掉队，说明整机厂这一轮在消化库存、而不是在拉新的减速器。**对送件来的客户，这意味着两件事：一是如果你的零件配套的是减速器厂，未来一个季度的排产会比上半年松；二是如果你的零件直接配套整机结构件（骨架、外壳、法兰），订单反而更紧。** 判断自己站在哪一边，比判断行业好不好更重要。

**来源**：国家统计局《2026 年 8 月份规模以上工业生产主要数据》及工业司首席统计师孙晓解读，2026-09-15；增速背离分析见腾讯新闻引海关总署、国家统计局数据报道，2026-09-16。

#### 2. 【机器人】2026-09-12 ｜ 优必选柳州"万台级"工厂投产：每 10 分钟下线 1 台

**摘要**
9 月 12 日，优必选在广西柳州的工业人形机器人超级智慧工厂正式投产，定位为全球首个适配万台级产能的人形机器人智能制造标杆工厂。工厂占地 1.4 万平方米、楼高 13.8 米，主要生产 Walker S 与 Cruzr 系列，设计节拍为**每 10 分钟下线 1 台**，年规划产能超万台。工厂由优必选与西门子联合打造，基于联合研发的 Yanshee MOM 制造运营管理系统实现工单排产、物料配送、质量监控全链路数字化，并依托机器人唯一 SN 序列号做到"一物一码"溯源。值得机加人注意的是两个数字：单台人形机器人**超过 2000 颗螺丝、50 余种规格**；单台整机测试时长**超过 4 小时**。

**对零件意味着什么**
"每 10 分钟一台"是节拍，但节拍只有在来料完全一致时才成立——2000 颗螺丝、50 多种规格、4 小时测试，这套体系对入口端零件的批次一致性要求是车规级的。**换句话说：这一轮客户不会再因为"首件漂亮"给你下单，他们会因为"第 200 件和第 1 件一样"给你下单。** 如果你的图纸上只有关键尺寸有公差、其余写"未注公差按 GB/T 1804-m"，那正是装配线上最容易出问题的地方。送件之前把未注公差也标清楚，是我们这一季最想对客户说的一句话。

**来源**：经济日报，2026-09（工厂投产报道）；深圳特区报，2026-09-14；人民网广西频道，2026-09-13；柳州投资促进局网站，2026-09-16。

#### 3. 【机床业】2026-09-15 ｜ 8 月数控金属切削机床产量 +18.2%，"造零件的机器"自己在涨

**摘要**
国家统计局 8 月产品产量数据显示，中高端装备类产品稳步发展：铁路机车、民用钢质船舶、燃气轮机、**数控金属切削机床**产量分别增长 133.3%、38.0%、36.5%、**18.2%**。同期装备制造业增加值同比 +12.1%，占规上工业比重 38.3%（同比提高 2.2 个百分点）。结构上，1—8 月新动能（高技术制造业、数字产品制造业等）对规上工业增加值增长的贡献率超五成，比 1—7 月提升超过 2 个百分点。对照同期出口：8 月规上工业出口交货值 1.4 万亿元，同比 +11.1%；1—8 月机电产品出口 12.91 万亿元，增长 21.9%（海关总署）。

**对零件意味着什么**
数控机床产量增速接近 20%，说明下游在扩产能——但扩的是**能加工复杂件的产能，不是通用三轴**。这里有一条很直白的推论：当你的竞争对手都在买五轴时，靠三轴多次装夹压价的空间会越来越小，而**按件的公差能力和交期会重新变成定价权**。对送件来的客户，这一条的实际含义是——今年谈价时，"我给你便宜 10%"越来越难要到，但"我能把交期从 3 周压到 10 天、并且第 10 件和第 1 件一样"越来越值钱。

**来源**：国家统计局工业司首席统计师孙晓解读 2026 年 8 月工业生产数据，2026-09-15；海关总署 1—8 月进出口数据（转引《中国机械工业年鉴》），2026-09。

#### 4. 【算力/液冷】2026-09-15 ｜ 首部液冷国标落定，冷板与快接头第一次有了"统一尺子"

**摘要**
推荐性国家标准 **GB/T 48023-2026《数据中心冷板式液冷系统技术规范》** 已于 2026 年 7 月 30 日发布，**2027 年 2 月 1 日实施**；第二部国标 **GB/T 48097-2026《数据中心冷板式液冷机柜通用规范》** 于 2026 年 8 月 28 日发布，2027 年 3 月 1 日实施。两部标准由全国信息技术标准化技术委员会归口，80 余家产学研用单位联合编制（含曙光数创、中国电子技术标准化研究院、华为数字能源、阿里云、腾讯、英维克、申菱环境、维谛技术等）。标准对机加件提出了可以直接对着图纸核的硬指标：**同规格冷板在额定流量下流阻误差 ≤ ±10%**；冷板耐压测试压降 **≤ 2%** 且不得变形破裂；**快速接头**插合状态不得有变形和可见液滴，密封性测试表面不得有气泡，且**不同制造商相同规格的插头插座宜能互换使用**；分集液器各分液口最大最小流量差 **≤ 平均值的 10%**。政策端同步：9 月 4 日中央网信办等七部门印发《促进数字化绿色化协同转型发展实施方案（2026—2030 年）》，明确支持液冷散热技术推广，并推动超百千瓦单机柜部署。

**对零件意味着什么**
这条对机加厂是本期最实在的一条。**"互换性"三个字一旦写进国标，冷板和快接头的竞争逻辑就从"谁能做"变成"谁能做得一样"**——同一规格不同批次的流阻必须落在 ±10% 以内，靠的不是单件精雕，而是夹具、刀具寿命管理和过程检验。**对送件来的客户，这句话翻译过来是：别再把液冷件当普通铝件发。** 请把流阻或流量要求、以及耐压测试条件写进图纸，因为从 2027 年 2 月起，你的客户会拿这把尺子来量你。冷板、Manifold、快接头本体、CDU 内部结构件——这些正是 5 轴铣、面形度严、还要保压不漏的那一类，量级通常落在 10–500 件。

**来源**：GB/T 48023-2026、GB/T 48097-2026 发布公告及标准解读（全国信标委 SAC/TC 28/SC 39 归口）；中央网信办等七部门《促进数字化绿色化协同转型发展实施方案（2026—2030 年）》，2026-09-04；科华数据、艾特网能参编公告，2026-09-14。

#### 5. 【机器人】2026-09-11 ｜ 上游在数钱：86 万件零件、100 万台电机，量产订单真的落地了

**摘要**
两条来自上市公司披露的一手数字，比任何论坛发言都硬。**长盈精密**（特斯拉 Optimus 一级供应商）在投资者关系活动中披露：2025 年全年交付人形机器人零组件约 **69 万件**，2026 年仅上半年就交付约 **86 万件**，半年超过去年全年；其具身智能与新兴科技硬件业务上半年营收 **2.43 亿元，同比 +537.64%**，产品覆盖灵巧手传动件、旋转执行器核心件、丝杠核心件、躯干大尺寸传动件和骨骼结构件，并已承接人形机器人整机组装订单。**雷赛智能** 2026 年半年报披露：无框力矩电机**在手订单超过 100 万台**，行星关节模组、谐波关节模组及高自由度灵巧手已获近百家主流机器人客户订单并规模化供货，公司正在建设年产 300 万台的自动化生产能力。同期优必选披露，2026 上半年全尺寸具身智能人形机器人销量 **921 台**（同比 +1946.7%），收入 5.90 亿元（同比 +1445%）；人形机器人总销量 16123 台。

**对零件意味着什么**
把"整机台数"和"零件件数"放在一起看，本期的关键结论就出来了：**整机的数字还很小（921 台），零件的数字已经很大（86 万件）。** 一台人形机器人有几百个机加零件，921 台对应的就是几十万件——这就是为什么零件端先赚到钱。**对送件来的客户：如果你还在等"整机放量"才动手打样，你已经晚了半年；订单已经在零件这一层发生了。** 反过来说，如果你的件是机器人结构件、关节壳体、法兰、支架，现在这个窗口期拼的不是报价，是**能不能在 10–50 件的小批量上把一致性守住**——因为客户的整条产线都建立在"第 N 件等于第 1 件"这个假设上。

### 我们怎么写这个栏目

**多久更新一次？** 每周一次。不做新闻刷屏——只放那些会改变你本季度该往哪投零件的条目。

**数据从哪来？** 只用一手来源：国家统计局、海关总署、中国机床工具工业协会、企业公告、有署名的报道。不把券商观点当事实搬。

**为什么写"厂里看到的事"，不是市场报告？** 因为市场报告告诉你风往哪边吹，厂里视角告诉你活到底有没有真的落地。每条新闻我们都附一句"对零件意味着什么"——这一句是我们负责的。

### Five Stories

#### 1. [Robotics] 2026-09-15 | Industrial robots up 34.6% in August — but the real signal is in the reducers

**Summary**
The National Bureau of Statistics released August industrial production data on September 15: industrial robot output rose **34.6%** YoY, while robot reducer output rose **11.4%**. Those two lines used to move together — reducer output grew 63.9% in 2025 and 57.3% in H1 2026, both well ahead of the 28.0% recorded for industrial robots over the same period. In July and August, however, reducer growth fell to 22.7% and then 11.4%, while complete-machine growth rose from 30.2% to 34.6%. Even stripping out base effects, August's two-year average reducer growth of roughly 19.7% still trails the 24.1% for complete machines. The NBS also reported that China's share of global humanoid robot shipments has risen to **97%**; MIIT expects full-year humanoid output to exceed 100,000 units and full-year above-scale industrial robot output to break 1 million units for the first time.

**What it means for your parts**
A reducer is the one precision assembly in a joint that has to ship as a matched set — housing, flexspline, circular spline, output flange. Four parts; if any one drifts dimensionally, the set won't assemble. Its output growth falling behind means machine builders are working through inventory this cycle, not pulling new reducers. **For anyone sending us parts, that cuts two ways: if your parts feed a reducer maker, expect a looser schedule next quarter than in H1. If your parts go directly into robot structure — skeletons, housings, flanges — the work gets tighter, not looser.** Knowing which side of that line you sit on matters more than knowing whether the industry is “good.”

**Source**: NBS, *Main Industrial Production Data for August 2026* and the interpretation by Sun Xiao, Chief Statistician of the NBS Industrial Department, Sep 15 2026; divergence analysis citing NBS and General Administration of Customs data via Tencent News, Sep 16 2026.

#### 2. [Robotics] 2026-09-12 | UBTECH's Liuzhou “10,000-unit” plant goes live: one robot off the line every 10 minutes

**Summary**
On September 12, UBTECH's industrial humanoid super factory in Liuzhou, Guangxi, officially began production — positioned as the world's first humanoid manufacturing flagship built for 10,000-unit-class capacity. The plant covers 14,000 m² across a 13.8 m building, produces the Walker S and Cruzr series, runs a designed takt of **one robot every 10 minutes**, and is planned for over 10,000 units a year. It was built jointly with Siemens on a co-developed Yanshee MOM manufacturing operations system, giving full-chain digital control over scheduling, material flow and quality monitoring, with a unique SN per robot enabling item-level traceability. Two numbers matter most to a machinist: each humanoid carries **more than 2,000 screws in over 50 specifications**, and each unit's final test runs **over 4 hours**.

**What it means for your parts**
“One every 10 minutes” is the takt — but a takt only holds if incoming parts are identical. 2,000 screws, 50-plus specs, 4 hours of testing: that stack imposes automotive-grade batch consistency on everything entering the plant. **In other words, this cycle a customer will not award you work because your first article was beautiful. They'll award it because article 200 measures like article 1.** If your drawing specifies tolerances on key dimensions and leaves everything else to “general tolerance per GB/T 1804-m,” that is precisely the region where an assembly line fails. Filling in your general tolerances before you send the drawing is the single thing we'd most like to ask of customers this quarter.

**Source**: Economic Daily, Sep 2026 (plant commissioning); Shenzhen Special Zone Daily, Sep 14 2026; People's Daily Online Guangxi, Sep 13 2026; Liuzhou Investment Promotion Bureau, Sep 16 2026.

#### 3. [CNC industry] 2026-09-15 | CNC metal-cutting machine tool output up 18.2% — the machines that make parts are being bought

**Summary**
NBS August product data shows mid- and high-end equipment advancing steadily: output of railway locomotives, civilian steel ships, gas turbines and **CNC metal-cutting machine tools** rose 133.3%, 38.0%, 36.5% and **18.2%** respectively. Equipment manufacturing value-added rose 12.1% YoY, reaching 38.3% of above-scale industry — up 2.2 percentage points. On the export side: August above-scale industrial export delivery value reached ¥1.4 trillion, up 11.1% YoY, while January–August mechanical and electrical product exports totaled ¥12.91 trillion, up 21.9% (General Administration of Customs).

**What it means for your parts**
Machine tool output growing near 20% means downstream is expanding capacity — but the capacity being added handles **complex parts, not general three-axis work**. The inference is blunt: when every competitor is buying five-axis, the room to win on multi-setup three-axis pricing keeps shrinking, and **tolerance capability and lead time become the pricing power again**. For anyone sending us parts, the practical read is this — this year, “I'll do it 10% cheaper” is getting harder to sell, while “I can pull the lead time from three weeks to ten days, and article 10 measures like article 1” is getting more valuable.

#### 4. [Compute / Liquid cooling] 2026-09-15 | China's first liquid-cooling national standard lands — cold plates and quick connectors finally get one ruler

**Summary**
The recommended national standard **GB/T 48023-2026, *Technical Specification for Cold Plate Liquid Cooling Systems in Data Centers***, was issued on July 30, 2026 and takes effect **February 1, 2027**. A second standard, **GB/T 48097-2026, *General Specification for Cold Plate Liquid Cooling Racks in Data Centers***, was issued August 28, 2026 and takes effect March 1, 2027. Both are administered by the National Technical Committee for Information Technology Standardization and were drafted jointly by more than 80 industry, academic and research organizations (including Sugon Digital, CESI, Huawei Digital Power, Alibaba Cloud, Tencent, Envicool, Shenling and Vertiv). The standards set hard, drawing-checkable targets for machined parts: **for cold plates of the same specification at rated flow, flow-resistance variation must be ≤ ±10%**; pressure testing must show **pressure drop ≤ 2%** with no deformation or rupture; **quick connectors** must show no deformation or visible droplets when mated, with no bubbles in sealing tests, and **plugs and sockets of the same specification from different manufacturers should be interchangeable**; manifold branch flow spread must be **≤ 10% of the arithmetic mean**. On policy: on September 4, the Cyberspace Administration of China and six other departments issued the *Implementation Plan for Promoting Coordinated Digital and Green Transformation (2026–2030)*, explicitly supporting liquid-cooling deployment and pushing toward 100 kW-plus single-rack densities.

**What it means for your parts**
This is the most concrete item this issue for a machine shop. **Once “interchangeability” is written into a national standard, cold plates and quick connectors stop competing on “who can make it” and start competing on “who can make it the same, every time”** — flow resistance within ±10% across batches is not won by sculpting one perfect part; it's won by fixtures, tool-life management and in-process inspection. **For anyone sending us parts, the translation is: stop treating liquid-cooling parts as ordinary aluminum.** Put the flow-resistance or flow-rate requirement, and the pressure-test condition, on the drawing — because from February 2027 your customers will measure you with this ruler. Cold plates, manifolds, connector bodies and CDU internal hardware are exactly the 5-axis, tight-flatness, leak-tested class of work, and the volumes typically sit in the 10–500 piece band.

**Source**: GB/T 48023-2026 and GB/T 48097-2026 issue announcements and standard interpretations (administered by SAC/TC 28/SC 39); CAC and six other departments, *Implementation Plan for Promoting Coordinated Digital and Green Transformation (2026–2030)*, Sep 4 2026; Kehua Data and Aiteanneng co-drafting announcements, Sep 14 2026.

**Откуда берутся цифры?** Только первоисточники: Национальное бюро статистики, Главное таможенное управление, Китайская ассоциация станкостроителей и инструментальщиков (CMTBA), отчётность компаний и именная пресса. Мы не перепечатываем аналитические заметки как факты.

**Почему взгляд цеха, а не рыночный отчёт?** Потому что рыночный отчёт говорит, куда дует ветер. Взгляд цеха говорит, приземлилась ли работа на самом деле. У каждой новости здесь есть строка «что это значит» — за неё мы и отвечаем.
