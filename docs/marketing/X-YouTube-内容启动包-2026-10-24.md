## 一、X（Twitter）第一批 5 条（复制即发）

**发帖时间**：北京时间**晚上 9–11 点**（正好是美国东部早上 9–11 点，工程师上班刷手机的时间）。每周 2–3 条，宁缺毋滥。

### 帖 1（品牌定位，首发）
```
We're a CNC machining factory in Shenzhen — 30 machines in-house, including 5-axis.

What we actually sell:
• MOQ 1 piece, no markup for small batches
• Quotes within 2 hours
• ±0.005 mm on critical features

Send a drawing, see for yourself: https://eternalcnc.com
```
> 角度：开门见山报三个硬数字。国外买家对"工厂自营 + 小批量友好"最敏感。

### 帖 2（过程故事，配 1–2 张加工中实拍）
```
A drawing came in at 9:14 this morning.

Titanium bracket, 5-axis, two features at ±0.005 mm.

Quote went out at 10:47. That's the whole trick — no sales layers, engineers quote directly.

(2h quote is our standard, not our exception.)
```
> 角度：讲故事比吹参数可信。时间线写具体分钟数是这类帖子的经典写法。把时间换成你真实的一单。

### 帖 3（知识型，配一张粗糙度对比/零件特写）
```
"Ra 0.4" on a drawing — what does that actually cost you?

Ra 1.6–3.2: standard as-machined finish, basically free
Ra 0.8–1.6: fine machining, minor cycle time adder
Ra 0.4–0.8: precision passes, real cost adder
Ra 0.1–0.4: grinding territory, plan for it early

Spec tighter than you need = paying for nothing.
```
> 角度：帮工程师省钱的科普帖，最容易被转发/收藏。这条同时是官网表面处理页的引流种子。

### 帖 4（材料冷知识，配铝屑/棒料照片）
```
6061-T6 vs 7075-T6, the version machinists actually care about:

7075: ~2x stronger, worse corrosion resistance, more expensive
6061: weldable, cheaper, plenty for most fixtures & enclosures

Rule of thumb: unless the part is loaded, 6061 saves your customer money — and telling them that earns the next order.
```
> 角度：站在买家立场讲省钱，塑造"值得信任的供应商"人设。

### 帖 5（设备实拍，配 5 轴或车床视频）
```
Weekend shift on the DMU-400.

One setup, five faces, cumulative error stays where it should — down.

Impellers, turbine housings, curved brackets: the parts that punish fixture stacks are the parts we like.
```
> 角度：设备帖不写参数堆砌，写"这台机器擅长解决什么问题"。视频比照片互动率高 3–5 倍。

**X 运营三不要**（新手最容易踩的坑）：
1. 不要每条都带链接（算法会限流）——5 条里带 1–2 条链接就够
2. 不要买粉丝、不要互关刷量（会永久伤害账号权重）
3. 不要中文/英文混发，账号定位就是英文
