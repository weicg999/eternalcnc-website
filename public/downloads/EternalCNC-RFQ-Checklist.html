Eternal CNCRFQ Submission Checklist

Attach the following when you send your drawing. A complete package lets us return a detailed quote within 2 hours. You can also submit online at /contact/get-a-quote — the wizard collects the same items in 3 steps.

Must-have (required)

  • Drawing file REQUIRED
    STEP / IGES / DWG / PDF / SAT. 3D model preferred; 2D PDF acceptable with dimensions called out.
  • Quantity REQUIRED
    Target qty per order, or a range (e.g. 50 / 200 / 1,000+). Tell us if it is a one-off or ongoing production.
  • Contact email REQUIRED
    So we can send the quote and DFM feedback.
  • Material REQUIRED (or "as per drawing")
    Mark "as per drawing" if the material is called out on the file, or specify a grade (e.g. 6061-T6, 304 SS, 7075).

Helpful (speeds up quoting)

  • Critical tolerances OPTIONAL
    Flag the features that must hold tight tolerance (fit surfaces, bores, datums). Standard is ISO 2768-f.
  • Surface finish / treatment OPTIONAL
    Anodizing, passivation, plating, powder coat, Ra target, etc.
  • Lead time needed OPTIONAL
    Your target ship date or simply "ASAP". Rush (3–5 days) available.
  • Application / industry OPTIONAL
    Aerospace, medical, robotics, automotive… helps us pick the right process and certs.
  • Special requirements OPTIONAL
    Certs (EN 10204 3.1), 100% inspection, packaging, marking, RoHS, etc.
Tip: the more of the "helpful" items you provide, the more accurate the quote and lead time. If a field is open, our engineers will propose it during DFM review — no need to block your RFQ.
