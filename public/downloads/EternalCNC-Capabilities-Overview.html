4. Quality Process

  1. DFM review — engineers check drawings for machinability, tolerance stack-up and cost before quoting.
  2. In-process verification — critical dimensions checked mid-run with our 2.5D measuring unit and precision gauges.
  3. First-article & sampling — 2.5D inspection per AQL; dimensional report available on request.
  4. Calibration — all machines calibrated quarterly; spindle runout re-checked before each run.
  5. Traceability — raw stock to finished part, with material certs on request.
